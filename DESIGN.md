## Brand & Style

The design system projects architectural precision, uncompromising execution, and absolute client reassurance in the comprehensive renovation and high-end remodeling sector. The visual language blends contemporary corporate rigor with Mediterranean architectural warmth—pairing heavy structural contrasts with airy, luminous negative space.

### Target Audience & Psychology
The primary users are high-intent homeowners, property investors, and commercial landlords seeking turnkey renovation management. The interface must mitigate the inherent anxiety of construction delays, budget overruns, and logistical stress. Every screen builds trust through radical clarity, transparent workflow previews, visible licensing guarantees, and prominent direct conversion paths.

### Aesthetic Direction
A modern architectural aesthetic grounded in crisp geometry, confident structural framing, and tactile digital cards. Generous vertical breathing room offsets dense project portfolios and technical cost breakdowns, creating an experience that feels as meticulously engineered as the physical spaces being renovated.

## Colors

The palette establishes an unwavering standard of executive quality through deep oceanic slate and structural charcoal, contrasted against high-visibility conversion accents designed to maximize contact velocity.

- **Primary (`#0f2a42` - Petrol Slate):** Anchors headers, critical structural boundaries, hero sections, and authoritative credentials. It conveys longevity, engineering rigor, and premium execution.
- **Secondary (`#ea580c` - Energetic Terracotta):** Strictly reserved for primary conversion drivers ("Pedir presupuesto", "Calcular coste"). Its warm contrast triggers immediate visual attention without feeling alarming.
- **Tertiary (`#128C7E` - Direct Contact Teal / WhatsApp):** Applied to floating contact docks, live consultation triggers, and verified customer service indicators.
- **Neutral (`#1e293b` - Charcoal Stone):** Primary text and iconography color, maintaining high typographic contrast without the harshness of pure black.
- **Surfaces (`#ffffff`, `#f8fafc`, `#f1f5f9`):** Alternate in rhythm across horizontal viewport sections to separate architectural services, step-by-step methodologies, and social proof metrics without relying on hard line dividers.

## Typography

The type system is powered by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, providing crisp geometric structure with modern terminal cuts. It balances contemporary European corporate elegance with warmth.

### Hierarchy & Scale Rules
- **Display & Headline Levels:** Heavy weights (700-800) with tight tracking tighten headline presence, creating clear focal points across conversion landing screens.
- **Editorial Balance:** Body copy maintains a generous 1.5x to 1.55x line height ratio to guarantee effortless scanning across multi-paragraph service scopes, project guarantees, and timeline breakdowns.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Used for trust badges, verified metrics, certifications, and form input annotations. Subtle positive letter spacing ensures legibility at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid that locks at an ultra-wide max content container of `1280px` to maintain optimal viewing lines on larger monitors.

### Breakpoint Strategy
- **Mobile (`< 640px`):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer margins. Content blocks, pricing tiers, and form fields stack into a single vertical sequence.
- **Tablet (`640px - 1024px`):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters. Project transformation galleries shift into 2-column comparison grids.
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** 12-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters. Quote estimation forms and contact summaries sit in dedicated right-hand sticky rails alongside detailed service breakdowns.

### Vertical Cadence
Full-width marketing bands alternate between white (`#ffffff`) and slate-white (`#f8fafc`). Section-to-section padding uses generous spacing (`4rem` to `6rem` on desktop, `2.5rem` to `3.5rem` on mobile) to give before-and-after imagery architectural presence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses ambient architectural shadows layered over ultra-subtle perimeter borders. Instead of heavy drop-shadows, components mimic soft daylight washing over smooth plaster.

### Shadow Scale
- **Flat Surface (`elevation-0`):** `0 0 0 1px rgba(15, 42, 66, 0.06)` — used for standard unselected cards and static informational panels.
- **Floating Card (`elevation-1`):** `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 42, 66, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 42, 66, 0.03)` with a hairline border `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`. Used for interactive project cards, trust review modules, and service selector blocks.
- **Raised Interactive (`elevation-2`):** `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 42, 66, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 42, 66, 0.04)`. Applied to hover states on interactive cards, modals, and sticky top navigation bars.
- **Conversion Accent Lift (`elevation-cta`):** `0 8px 24px -2px rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.35)`. Dedicated exclusively to secondary accent action buttons to visually pull them off the screen plane.
- **WhatsApp Persistent Float (`elevation-wa`):** `0 8px 28px -2px rgba(18, 140, 126, 0.40)`. Ensures the primary live messaging channel remains clearly accessible above all interactive layers.

## Shapes

The design system implements balanced rounded corners to soften heavy architectural and construction data without looking playful or juvenile.

### Radius Assignments
- **Micro Radii (`0.375rem` / `6px`):** Checkboxes, form status pips, and inline data tags.
- **Component Radii (`0.5rem` to `0.75rem` / `8px - 12px`):** Input fields, standard dropdown buttons, and secondary badges.
- **Surface Radii (`1rem` to `1.5rem` / `16px - 24px`):** Main portfolio cards, budget calculation containers, testimonial cards, and conversion modal dialogs.
- **Pill Radii (`9999px`):** Contact chips, WhatsApp persistent action anchors, category filters, and guarantee tags ("Presupuesto Cerrado", "Garantía 5 Años").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Conversion Button ("Pedir Presupuesto Gratuito"):** Solid `#ea580c` fill, white bold typography (`label-lg`), pill or rounded-xl geometry, with `elevation-cta`. Hover elevates brightness and expands shadow reach without changing scale.
- **Secondary Corporate Button ("Ver Proyectos Realizados"):** Border `1.5px solid #0f2a42`, transparent surface, `#0f2a42` text. Hover shifts background to `#0f2a42` with crisp white text.
- **WhatsApp Direct Action:** Solid `#128C7E` or `#25D366` background, pill shape, white text, accompanied by the official chat icon. Persistent desktop and mobile positioning in the lower-right viewport.

### Cards
- **Project Showcase Card:** Full bleed high-resolution interior photography with a `16px` border radius, overlaying a subtle before/after pill badge. Lower compartment carries architectural project parameters: location (e.g., "Eixample, Barcelona"), square meterage, turnaround time, and total renovation scope.
- **Trust & Guarantee Badges:** Minimalist surface cards in `#ffffff` with hairline `#e2e8f0` borders. Iconography rendered in primary `#0f2a42` alongside bold validation metrics (e.g., "Colegiados Oficiales", "Seguro RC 1.000.000€", "Presupuesto sin Desvíos").

### Form Fields & Quotation Funnel
- **Text Inputs & Selectors:** Clean white inputs framed by `1px solid #cbd5e1` with generous internal padding (`0.875rem 1rem`). On focus, border transitions directly to `#0f2a42` with an ambient glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(15, 42, 66, 0.12)`.
- **Property Type Toggles:** Segmented card-style selectors allowing users to choose "Piso Completo", "Cocina & Baños", or "Local Comercial" using simple vector blueprints that turn primary slate when selected.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square rounded (`6px`) checks and circular radios built with thick active fills in `#0f2a42`, ensuring immediate validation feedback during multi-step cost estimation.
